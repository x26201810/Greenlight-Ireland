One journey. Different starting points.

The report identifies four developmental stages. “Find your family fit” uses these to make the choice feel personal.

6–9 Understand Earning, spending, saving, needs vs wants, simple goals.
10–12 Manage Budgeting, digital payments, scams, saving goals, trade-offs.
13–15 Decide Interest, credit, subscriptions, investing, financial scams.
16–17 Prepare Payslips, tax, loans, rent, investing, resilience.

Questions parents will ask.

Why should I pay when Revolut <18 can be free?

The proposition is not simply access to a card. The differentiation is a family learning layer: age-based content, goals, earning, saving and parent-child reinforcement.

What if I have children of different ages?

The qualifier is designed around the family rather than one child. The recommended tier can support the household while the experience adapts learning to each child's stage.

Is this replacing school financial education?

No. The strategy recommends the opposite: school teaches concepts; the product provides practice; home reinforces it.

Are the prices final?

No. €0, €4.99/month and €7.99/month are proposed indicative tiers from the supplied strategy report and would require commercial validation.
